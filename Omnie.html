About me

Data analysis, BI solutions and data visualisation.

Working with data

Working with data has been among my interests since my studies in geodesy and cartography, where I learned the principles and methods of presenting data on maps, how spatial analysis works, and how to work with photogrammetric imagery and remote-sensing data. Today I specialise in analysing large datasets with SQL and Python. I work effectively with project teams in finance, controlling and HR, implement BI solutions and use data visualisation tools. My goal is not only precision and accuracy in data analysis but, above all, creating real value for organisations through proper work with data.

I am certified by Microsoft (Power BI Data Analyst, Power Platform Fundamentals, Fabric Data Engineer) and hold the Claude Certified Associate – Foundations credential. You will find the full list of certificates below.
